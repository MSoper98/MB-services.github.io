Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. We provide free consultations and estimates for projects.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured for residential and commercial work.

Do you write the CL-100 letter for termites?

Yes we do! We are specialized in Termite and Water damage!

Can I provide photos of my project?

Absolutely. Photos help us better understand your needs before your consultation. You can upload project images through our contact form.

How long does remodeling usually take?

Every project is different. Timelines depend on scope, materials, and permitting.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When required, we obtain permits and coordinate inspections.

How do I get started with my project?

Simply contact us through our estimate request form, provide details about your project, and we will follow up to discuss your next steps.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, and major credit cards.

What areas do you typically service?

We have a section on the website dedicated to giving customers that exact answer! Check our "Service Areas" tab to find our list of cities we've previously serviced. If you don't see your town or city listed, send us a message!
